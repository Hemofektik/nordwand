Nordwand

                        
Bow the body, and climb when the black breath ring sits on the white mark. A tight ring is a perfect move.

                        
Phone

                        
Lean — hold the pad. Right bows in toward the rock, left bows out into the air.

                        
Climb — tap on the beat. Tapping the wall does the same thing.

                        
Let go — release every hold. Only the rope is left.

                        
Pinch — two fingers on the picture zoom in or out, one pixel at a time. Fingers on the pad or the buttons stay on those controls.

                        
Keyboard

                        
A / D or the arrow keys lean. W or Up climbs. F lets go. P pauses, R builds a new wall, M mutes, H is this help, N the next song. The mouse wheel zooms. The picture stays on the pixel grid; resizing the window picks a new whole-pixel scale.
